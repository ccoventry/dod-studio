SPECTATING (HLTV demos)
  ds_spec_match_pov 0/1
      Make a spectated first-person view match the player's own recording: gun animations, lost gunshots, the crosshair, prone eye height, the gun lowering.
  ds_spec_lock 0/1
      Keep the camera on the player you are watching when they die.
  ds_spec_deathcam 0/1
      When the player you are watching dies, show the death cam their own recording shows (2.4 s behind their body, turning to the killer), then their eyes again.
  ds_spec_target <player>
      Put the camera on a player (number 1-32; ds_hud_deathmsg players lists them).
  ds_spec_hide_bars 0/1
      Hide the spectator bars and everything on them.
  ds_spec_hide_text 0/1
      Hide the camera mode's name ('Free Look', ...) and the green 'Press DUCK for Spectator Menu' (POV deaths too).
  ds_spec_hide_hltv_messages 0/1
      Hide the HLTV proxy's on-screen messages.

HUD
  ds_hud_hide_scoreboard 0/1
      Stop a POV demo's recorded TAB presses showing the scoreboard.
  ds_hud_hide_crosshair 0/1
      Hide the crosshair and keep it hidden.
  ds_hud_hide_voice_menus 0/1
      Hide the numbered voice-command and hand-signal menus a POV demo's player opened.
  ds_hud_hide_element <name> 0/1
      Hide one HUD element; the bare command lists them.
  ds_hud_hide_map_text 0/1
      Hide the text a map puts on screen itself (mortar warnings, round results).
  ds_hud_hide_clan_text 0/1
      Hide clan-match text: warm-up and ready rules, Warmup Mode, the countdown, MATCH IS LIVE!
  ds_hud_hide_center_text 0/1
      Hide the text the server prints in the middle of the screen (plugin countdowns and notices).
  ds_map_hide_asset list|add|del|clear <model|hud.txt name>...
      Hide an asset (sprite, model) by its file path, like mirv_matte_entities.
  ds_hud_deathmsg
      Kill feed: max lines, offset, block players, fake a kill, font <face> <size> [bold] [shadow], scale <1..8> (both live). The bare command shows the options.
  ds_hud_objectives
      Move the objective icons and the objective timer, or draw them bigger: scale <1..8>, and count_scale <1..8> for the player count under a flag. The bare command shows the options.
  ds_hud_overview_layout
      Move or resize the overview map.
  ds_hud_overview_players 0-4
      Which players the overview map shows: 0 the game's own, 1 everyone, 2 the other team, 3 the watched player, 4 nobody.
  ds_hud_overview_team_marker 0-2
      The camera icon on the overview map: 0 the game's own, 1 their team's icon in HLTV too, 2 none.
  ds_hud_overview_hide_speaker 0/1
      Leave the speaker icon off the players on the overview map.
  ds_hud_show_scope 0/1
      Show the sniper scope while a demo plays; in-eye it is the watched player's rifle.

VIEW (POV demos: the recording player's own view)
  ds_view_use_cl_bob 0/1
      POV demos: the view and gun bob by your cl_bob, cl_bobcycle and cl_bobup, as in live play (0: the bob the demo recorded, the recording player's own).
  ds_view_hide_headshot_red_screen 0/1
      No red screen when the player is hit in the head.
  ds_view_hide_screen_shake 0/1
      No screen shake from grenades going off nearby.

PLAYERS
  ds_player_change_name add id <slot|SteamID|self> [in <demo>,...] to <name>
      Rename a player everywhere their name shows (kill feed, scoreboard, chat), up to 63 characters; also list, remove <n>, clear.
  ds_player_hide_hand_signals 0/1
      Replace players' hand-signal animations.
  ds_player_fix_model_flicker 0/1
      HLTV demos: strafing players' models no longer flicker round for a frame when the demo clock steps back (on by default).
  ds_player_overrides 0/1
      Sprays and crosshairs in demos follow DoD Studio's Players table (Configuration > Players); 0 is the game's own. Studio sets it.
  ds_player_spray [<name>|players]
      Make logos\<name>.png your spray for live play (writes tempdecal.wad, shows from the next connect); bare lists the PNGs; players shows what Studio's Players table gives each player in the demo.

MAP
  ds_map_clear_decals
      Clear every decal (bullet holes, blood, sprays).
  ds_map_hide_asset list|add|del|clear <model|hud.txt name>...
      Hide an asset (sprite, model) by its file path, like mirv_matte_entities.
  ds_map_allow_shaders 0/1
      25th Anniversary only: allow the engine's map-surface shaders during playback.

SOUND
  ds_sound_mute_voice_commands 0/1
      Silence voice commands ('fire in the hole!' and the rest).
  ds_sound_mute_victory_music 0/1
      Silence the music the map plays when a team wins the round.
  ds_sound_mute_flag_capture 0/1
      Silence the map's point-captured announcements, and also its objective-secured ones (dod_escape's).

DEMO PLAYBACK
  ds_demo_seek_to <time>
      Jump viewdemo playback to a time on the VCR bar's clock: 610, 10:10 or 1:02:03.
      Typed on the same line as viewdemo, it waits until the demo has loaded.
  ds_demo_seek_by <time>
      Jump forward (or back, with a minus) by that much: 30, 1:30 or -0:30.
  ds_demo_seek_skip_between 0/1
      A jump skips the events and commands it passes over.
  ds_demo_fast_load 0/1/<ms>
      viewdemo loads the demo up to 8 ms a frame (1, the default) or <ms>, not 33 packets a frame (0).
  ds_demo_other_drives 0/1
      viewdemo and playdemo open a demo on another drive by its full path: viewdemo "D:/folder/demo".
  ds_demo_reload
      Play the last demo again.
  ds_demo_review yes|maybe|no|replay|next|back|stop
      Answer the highlight DoD Studio's Review highlights is playing, or move through them (the Review tab).
  ds_demo_ex_interp_max <ms>
      Raise the engine's 100 ms ceiling on ex_interp.

CAPTURE
  ds_capture_batch start "<file>"|away|stop
      Run a DoD Studio capture batch on the original demos, seeking between clips (Studio sends this).
  ds_capture_schedule load "<file>"|clear
      Run the commands in a schedule file at their demo times, from the hook (#434, R&D).
  ds_capture_run_in_background 0/1
      Run at full speed while another window is in front, so a capture batch doesn't need the screen.
  ds_capture_stay_behind 0/1
      Keep the game behind your other windows while it records, instead of coming to the front.

DOD STUDIO WINDOW
  ds_ui_panel [1|0|reset|<tab>]
      Open (1) or close (0) this window; bare toggles it; reset restores the default layouts; a tab's name (highlights) opens that tab.
  ds_ui_viewdemo_in_panel 0/1
      viewdemo opens this window on Playback instead of the VCR bar.
  ds_ui_console_in_panel 0/1
      The console key opens this window on the Console tab.
  ds_ui_resizable_windows 0/1
      Every game window can be resized by its edges.
  ds_ui_remember_layout 0/1
      Game windows come back where you left them.
  ds_ui_demo_list_folders 0/1
      The Load Demo window and the Demos tab also list and open folders.
  ds_ui_demo_list_hide_empty 0/1
      Folders with no demo anywhere inside them aren't listed.
  ds_ui_demo_list_count_subfolders 0/1
      A folder's demo count includes its subfolders.
  ds_ui_demo_list_show_counts 0/1
      Folders are counted, and folder rows on the Demos tab show their demo count. 0: nothing is counted.

HD TEXTURES
  ds_hd_enabled 0/1
      Load the HD textures built by DoD Studio's HD page.
  ds_hd_style <name>
      Which HD style to use (ultrasharp by default).

DEBUG
  ds_debug_status
      Print everything DoD Studio's hook is doing to the console.
  ds_debug_position
      Print where the view and the watched player are.
  ds_debug_msglog <name>...
      Log chosen game messages to the hook log.
  ds_debug_hd_misses
      List the textures that had no HD version, per map.
  ds_debug_log_weapon_model 0/1
      Log the weapon model the spectated player holds.
  ds_debug_log_gun_lowered 0/1
      Log each time the first-person gun goes down or comes back up, with the demo's clock (POV and HLTV in-eye). POV reads client.dll's own flag, not the drawn gun, so it misses jump lowering.
  ds_debug_log_spectator_target 0/1
      Log who the spectator camera follows.
  ds_debug_log_texture_loads 0/1
      Log every HD texture load.
  ds_debug_log_view_bob 0/1
      Log the live and recorded bob every frame (ds_view_use_cl_bob).
  ds_debug_log_server_sounds 0/1
      Log every sound the server sends, and whether ds_sound_mute_victory_music or ds_sound_mute_flag_capture muted it.
  ds_debug_keep_detail_cache 0/1
      Leave the engine's detail texture cache alone (the bug that switches detail off by position), to compare.
  ds_debug_cloak_instead 0/1
      While staying behind, a minimised game is cloaked in place instead of moved off-screen, to compare.
